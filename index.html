Design: Interactive Image Compression App

Sketching out an app that lets someone upload an image, pick a compression level k, and see the compressed result next to the original.

Why I laid it out this way

  • Sidebar stays put. I put upload, the k slider, and the stats all in one column on the left so you can keep dragging the slider around without losing your place or having to re-upload anything.
  • Numbered top to bottom. Upload, then pick k, then read the stats — that's the order I'd actually use it in, so that's the order the controls follow.
  • Original and compressed side by side, same size. I didn't want to make people flip between two views to judge quality, so both images sit next to each other at identical size.
  • Stats live right under the slider. Size reduction and error update in the same spot you just touched, so the trade-off between k and quality is obvious as you drag.
  • Demo image link. Not everyone wants to hunt for a photo first, so there's a one-click way to try the app before uploading anything.

I ended up building this as a real Shiny app afterward — see the project repository for the source and the live app link on Canvas.
