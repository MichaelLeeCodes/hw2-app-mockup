Design: Interactive Image Compression App

Sketching out an app that lets someone upload an image, pick a compression level k, and see the compressed result next to the original.

Why I laid it out this way

  • Sidebar stays put. I put upload, the k slider, and the stats all in one column on the left so you can keep dragging the slider around without losing your place or having to reupload anything.
  • Numbered top to bottom. Upload, then pick k, then read the stats.
  • Original and compressed side by side, same size. I didn't want to make people flip between two views to judge quality, so both images sit next to each other at identical size.
  • Stats live right under the slider. Size reduction and error update in the same spot you just touched, so the difference between k and quality changes as you drag.
  • Demo image link. It just makes it easier for someone to see what the app does before uploading a photo.
